Counting Rules & Data Notes

Source differences, exclusions, and verification.

Historical USC Schedule of Classes snapshot. These are historical enrollment counts currently published by USC, retrieved on the dates below. They are not certified census-date or final institutional headcounts.

  • Count each semester and section number once, under its scheduled ACAD, IDSN or PRIN prefix. Contextual cross-listings scheduled by other schools are excluded. Cancelled sections, missing counts, and nonpositive capacities are excluded.
  • Students Enrolled means one student enrolled in one course section. Students taking multiple courses or attending more than one semester are counted more than once.
  • Student Credit Hours / units = students enrolled × course units, for sections with exactly one published unit value. Variable-unit and unknown-unit sections are excluded from SCH, but remain in enrollment totals. Known zero-unit courses contribute zero SCH.
  • Available Seats means seats not filled: max(capacity − enrollment, 0) per section. Annual utilization is combined enrollment divided by combined capacity across all three semesters.
  • “Not offered” means no counted offering in that term snapshot. “Unavailable” means the term dataset could not be retrieved and no saved snapshot exists. Numeric zero means a reported zero. Annual totals require all three datasets.
  • Data is retrieved daily from USC’s public API and during Netlify builds. Retrieval dates do not establish when USC last changed a historical record. A retrieval older than 48 hours is marked outdated; failures retain a labeled saved snapshot.
